About Habeet

Habeet is a simple productivity app designed especially for students. We understand how overwhelming it can be to stay organized, manage tasks, and keep focus while balancing studies and personal life.

Our goal is to make habit tracking, planning, and focus feel lighter, more visual, and even a little enjoyable.

This app is currently still in development. It was created by first-year students as part of the Grand Challenges project.

Thank you for using Habeet 💛
